# Exvius Offline v0.1.16

- Panel quests and special challenges now count more of the actions they ask for, including ability awakenings, battle stats, chains, and Dark Visions and raid clears.
- Fixed blocked special challenge claims and Dark Visions milestones. Challenge rewards now include vision cards, unit points, and ability and spell unlocks where earned.
- Stat pots, doors, burst pots, Cactuars, and Gil Snappers give their value when converted. Trust moogles stay available for fusion.
- Equipment, materia, and vision-card favourites, profile edits, and read notices are saved.
- Playing Arena earns a weekly participation reward offline.
- Town merchants protect equipped items from sale. Archived exchange shops have English greetings and farewells.

Open the game and accept the Android update when prompted. The launcher also downloads a small game data update. Your campaign and existing downloads carry over.
