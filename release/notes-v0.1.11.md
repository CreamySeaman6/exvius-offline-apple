# v0.1.11

Tickets bought in the shop now appear in the correct summons. Rare tickets work on eligible single-summon banners, and NV EX tickets work on NV EXVIUS Summon. The NV EX summon also uses its NV-capable pool.

Your previously purchased tickets remain in your inventory. Legacy EX tickets still use the separate EXVIUS Summon.

Install over your existing app. Your save and downloaded game data stay in place.
