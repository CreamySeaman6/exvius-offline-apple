# Exvius Offline v0.1.18

- Clearing a story stage now saves the flags that unlock the next mission or dungeon. This fixes progression in Season 4 and several older side routes. Existing saves recover the missing flags for stages already cleared.
- The Town of Mitra now plays the original conversation after the forge. Rain and Lasswell discuss their next route before the follow-up scene.

Open the game while online and accept Android's update prompt. Your campaign and existing downloads carry over.
