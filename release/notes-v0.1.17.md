# Exvius Offline v0.1.17

- Get Neo Vision Kira by clearing Lv. 1 of **Vow of the Revived Legend** under **Vortex > Filter > Collab**. The event includes all eight original stages. Bring units from the Another World or FFBE S4 category.
- Kira has his original Brave Shift, equipment rewards, and combat animations. His profile, abilities, and event text are available in English.
- The original Beast King Gnon sprites, stage formations, and attack records were recovered. His missing stats, attack timing, and repeat drops were reconstructed for offline play. The new event banner uses original game artwork.
- Restored the Farplane town and mission rule data that could cause Vortex to close on entry.

Open the game to get the update. Accept Android's install prompt to keep your campaign and existing downloads.
